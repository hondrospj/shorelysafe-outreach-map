READING YOUR MAP

A click is a signal.

The map shows a recipient’s associated municipality, not their physical location when they click.

  • Clicks are recorded link events. They do not prove an attached video was watched.
  • Opens are a separate, less certain signal; image loading and privacy features can affect them.
  • Unattributed means the source could not name the recipient. Group emails and CCs can cause this.
  • Flagged automation is excluded from totals unless you choose to include it in the activity list.
